Service disclosures and data handling

Service availability, contractual terms, and deployment controls must be confirmed for your workload. This page is an engineering disclosure, not a completed privacy policy, service agreement, or compliance certification.

Company and products

Aaruni Tech brings together Aaruni Cloud, Syllovo Studio, and digital engineering advisory. Staff use a separate employee portal.

Availability and support

Cloud Compute, Containers, and Render require administrator access. Other release availability has not yet been verified. No blanket 99.99% uptime commitment or performance multiplier is established by this page. Ask for written availability, capacity, support, and recovery terms.

Hosting and sovereignty

The implementation uses Cloudflare Pages/Workers and D1, with additional configured storage and execution providers. A Hyderabad region identifier does not establish that every request, copy, backup, log, or provider operation stays in India.

Before production use, request the actual processing and backup locations, subprocessor list, retention and deletion schedule, encryption and key ownership details, and incident contact. “Sovereign” and “zero egress” must be defined in the applicable service terms rather than assumed from branding.

Personal data and voice

Use only voices you are authorized to clone. Confirm consent capture, provider processing, deletion, and retention before submitting voice recordings. Do not upload confidential employee, customer, or publisher materials until access controls and data terms are confirmed.

Inquiries

The contact form requests your email, organization, and message so the team can respond. Avoid sensitive data. Request correction or deletion through contact@aarunitech.com. A success message confirms storage only; it does not imply email delivery.

Security reports

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to contact@aarunitech.com. Do not include credentials or personal data in public issues.

Pricing and third parties

Storage fees, quotas, provider charges, and refund terms require a current quote or agreement. Third-party names describe integrations and do not establish partnership or endorsement. Trademark registration status is not asserted here.
